Sharing Your Screen to a Zoom Room

A quick guide for laptop users — Zoom desktop app
Updated Jul 31, 2026 4 min read

This guide shows you how to share your screen from your laptop to a physical Zoom Room using the Zoom desktop app — whether the room is detected automatically or you need to connect with a code.

Part 1 · In-room sharing Part 2 · Manual room code

Quick answer

Laptop near the room? Open Zoom, click the Room icon top‑right, then Share screen. Joining a scheduled meeting instead? Click ··· next to it and choose Join from room.

Part 1

Share Your Screen to a Zoom Room

Use this when your laptop is near a Zoom Room — a meeting room with its own camera, screen, and speakers — and you want to send your laptop screen straight to that room's display.

  1. 1

    Open the Zoom desktop app. On the home screen you'll see your upcoming meetings for the day.

  2. 2

    In the top‑right corner of the app, click the Room icon.

  3. 3

    From the menu that appears, choose one of the options: New meeting, Share screen, or Connect to room. Click Share screen to send your laptop screen straight to the nearby Zoom Room.

  4. 4

    For a scheduled meeting instead, click the ··· (more options) next to that meeting.

  5. 5

    Choose Join from room to join the meeting through the Zoom Room and share your screen there.

    Note: This option is only available if there is a Zoom link attached to the invite.
    Tip: If the Room icon or these options are grayed out, this feature has been disabled by your account admin. Contact IT/support if you need it enabled.
Part 2

If the Room Isn't Detected Automatically

Sometimes the Zoom desktop app can't automatically detect a nearby Zoom Room. When that happens, you can connect manually using a room code shown on the room's display.

  1. 6

    From your meeting on the home screen, click Start (or the ··· next to the meeting) and choose Start from room.

  2. 7

    If Zoom can't find the room automatically, a Room not detected pop‑up appears, asking for a room code.

  3. 8

    Look at the TV or display in the meeting room. The room code is shown in the top‑right corner of the screen.

  4. 9

    Type the room code into the field on your laptop and click Continue to connect.

    Tip: The room code changes each time the room screen resets, so always check the current code on the display rather than reusing an old one.

Cheat sheet

Room detected
  1. Open Zoom desktop app
  2. Click the Room icon
  3. Click Share screen
Room not detected
  1. Click Start → Start from room
  2. Read the code on the room display
  3. Type it in and click Continue
